Loom is widely used by Skool community owners for module walkthroughs, screen-share tutorials, and personalized feedback. When a Loom video is embedded inside a Skool lesson, right-clicking the player offers no save option, and not every lesson displays Loom's built-in download icon.

This guide explains how embedded Loom videos work inside Skool, how to check creator permissions first, and how to save a Loom recording you can already watch in your active session as a standard MP4 file.

## Quick Answer: How do I download a Loom video from Skool?

First check whether the creator enabled Loom's own download icon in the player toolbar. If not, and your community allows personal offline study copies, install **Video Saver for Skool** in Chrome, Edge, Brave, or Opera, open the Skool lesson so the Loom player loads, and click **Download** to save the video locally as an MP4.

## Step-by-Step: Save an Embedded Loom Video

Saving an embedded Loom lesson from Skool takes four steps on a desktop Chromium browser. Make sure the Loom iframe actually mounts in your logged-in Skool tab before clicking the extension button, since some lessons wrap the embed behind a cover image until you click play.

1. **Install the extension:** Add **Video Saver for Skool** from the Chrome Web Store to Chrome, Edge, Brave, or Opera on Windows, macOS, or Linux.
2. **Open the lesson or post:** Log in to Skool and navigate to the classroom lesson or community post containing the Loom recording.
3. **Mount the Loom player:** If the lesson displays a static thumbnail cover, click play for a second so the Loom iframe loads and the **Download** button appears.
4. **Click Download:** Click the **Download** button on the video (or click the extension icon in your browser toolbar), select your quality, and save the `.mp4` file (H.264 video with AAC audio).

## What If an Embedded Loom Is Private or Asks for a Password?

Video Saver for Skool only works on videos that your logged-in Skool session can actively play in the browser. It cannot bypass Loom passwords, private workspace access locks, or restricted embeds that refuse playback in your tab.

If a creator shared a lesson password in the text notes below the video, enter that password in the Loom player first so the video plays normally, and verify that your community permits personal offline downloads. Online "paste a Loom link" websites usually fail on private Skool embeds because their remote servers do not hold your Skool session or workspace permission.

## Respecting Creator Settings and Community Rules

Before using any browser extension on an embedded Loom, check whether the creator enabled Loom's native download icon in the player corner—if they did, use that built-in option first. Check your community's **About** tab or onboarding rules, ask the creator if you are unsure, and keep any personal offline study copy strictly private.
