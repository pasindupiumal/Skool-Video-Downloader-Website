Loom is widely used by Skool community owners for module walkthroughs, screen-share tutorials and personal feedback. When a Loom video is embedded in a Skool lesson, right-clicking it offers no save option, and many creators also switch off Loom's own download button in their workspace settings.

This guide explains how embedded Loom videos work inside Skool and how to save one you already have access to as a standard MP4 file.

## Quick Answer: How do I download a Loom video from Skool?

Install the **Video Saver for Skool** Chrome extension, open the Skool lesson or community post that contains the Loom video, and click the **Download** button on the player. The extension saves the video your browser is already playing as an MP4, including password-protected Looms once you have unlocked them.

## Step-by-Step: Save an Embedded Loom Video

1. **Install the extension:** Add **Video Saver for Skool** from the Chrome Web Store to Chrome, Brave, Microsoft Edge or Opera on a desktop computer.
2. **Open the lesson or post:** Log in to Skool and open the classroom lesson or community post with the Loom recording.
3. **Let the player load:** If the lesson shows a thumbnail cover, click it once so the Loom player appears.
4. **Click Download:** Click the **Download** button on the video (or open the extension icon in your toolbar), choose a quality and save the `.mp4` file.

## Password-Protected Loom Videos

Some creators put a password on a Loom and list it in the lesson notes. To save one:

1. Enter the password in the embedded Loom player so the video unlocks and plays.
2. Click **Download** and save the MP4.

You must already have the password and access to the lesson. The extension does not bypass passwords you do not know. Online "paste a Loom link" sites generally cannot handle these videos, because their servers have neither the password nor your logged-in Skool session.

## A Note on Creators' Download Settings

Loom lets workspace owners turn off downloads. Video Saver for Skool works from the video your browser is already playing, not from Loom's download button, so that setting does not stop it. But a disabled download option can be a sign that a creator wants lessons kept on the platform. Check your community's rules, keep any copy for your own study, and never share it.
