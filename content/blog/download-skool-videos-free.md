## What Counts Toward Your 5 Free Downloads?

Only completed video saves count toward your 5-download trial allowance. Failed downloads, dropped network connections, or cancelled transfers do not deduct from your 5 free downloads, and choosing a different resolution for one video counts as a single download once saved.

- **Completed saves:** Each finished MP4 download uses 1 of your 5 trial slots.
- **Failed or cancelled saves:** If a stream times out or you cancel mid-transfer, it does not count against your allowance.
- **Re-downloading the same lesson:** Saving the same lesson a second time (for example at 1080p after saving 720p) counts as a second completed download, so pick your preferred quality first.
