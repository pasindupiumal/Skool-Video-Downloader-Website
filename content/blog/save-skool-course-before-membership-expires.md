Paid Skool communities and cohort programs end. Subscriptions lapse, sprints close, and when your paid access ends you typically lose the classroom lessons, call replays and community posts you were relying on. If you want to keep a personal offline copy of what you paid to learn, the time to do it is while your access is still active.

This checklist walks through archiving a course before access ends, for your own offline study. It also works for creators backing up their own classrooms.

## Quick Answer: How do I save a Skool course before my membership expires?

Install the **Video Saver for Skool** Chrome extension while your membership is active, open each lesson in the **Classroom** tab and click **Download** to save it as an MP4. Then do the same for any important replays in the **Community** feed, organise the files by module, and save attachments such as PDFs yourself. Start several days before your access ends, because the extension only works while you can still play the videos.

## What Happens When Access Ends?

Exactly what happens depends on the community and its settings, but this is the usual pattern:

| Skool area | While you have access | After paid access ends | Can you archive it? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Classroom video lessons** | Stream in Skool | Typically locked | Yes, with the extension, before access ends |
| **Community call replays and posts** | Viewable in the feed | Typically locked in private or paid groups | Yes, open the post and click Download |
| **Lesson notes, PDFs and links** | Shown below each video | Typically locked | Save them yourself: download PDFs, copy notes |

**Important:** you cannot download lessons after access has ended. The extension uses your own logged-in session and only saves videos your account can play.

## Before You Start: Plan the Backup

- **Check your end date.** Find when your access ends in your Skool membership settings, and work back from it.
- **List what you want.** Count the courses and modules worth keeping. A few reference lessons are quick; a full curriculum takes real time.
- **Note any Loom passwords.** If lessons use password-protected Looms, copy the passwords from the lesson notes first.
- **Check your storage.** Video files are large. Make sure you have room on your computer or an external drive.
- **Pick a plan for the size of the job** (see below).

## The 5-Step Course Backup Checklist

### Step 1: Count the lessons and choose a plan
Open the **Classroom** tab and count what you want to keep. A handful of lessons may fit inside the [5 free downloads](/blog/download-skool-videos-free). A full course usually needs unlimited downloads, which means the Monthly plan or Lifetime Pro.

| Plan | Price | Fits |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Free** | $0, 5 downloads in total (one-time, per device) | A few key lessons |
| **Standard Monthly** | $20/month, unlimited, cancel anytime | Archiving one course in a month |
| **Lifetime Pro** | One-time payment, tiered ([current price](/#pricing)) | Several communities over time |

All purchases have a 14-day refund policy.

### Step 2: Set up a clean folder structure
Without numbering, hundreds of files sort alphabetically and scramble the order of the course. Use a two-digit prefix for each module and lesson:

```text
Skool-Course-Name/
├── 01-Foundations/
│   ├── 01-Welcome-and-Setup.mp4
│   ├── 02-Core-Framework.mp4
│   └── 02-Core-Framework-Worksheet.pdf
├── 02-Implementation/
│   ├── 01-Step-by-Step-Walkthrough.mp4
│   └── 02-Case-Study-Breakdown.mp4
└── 03-Coaching-Call-Replays/
    ├── 2026-10-15-Weekly-QA-Call.mp4
    └── 2026-10-22-Hotseat-Review.mp4
```

### Step 3: Download classroom lessons one module at a time
1. Open the first lesson in Chrome, Brave, Edge or Opera.
2. Let the video player load. Click play briefly if it uses a cover thumbnail.
3. Click the **Download** button on the video.
4. Choose a quality and save the MP4 into the right module folder.
5. Wait for the download to finish, then move on to the next lesson.

The extension handles Skool's native player, YouTube, Loom, Vimeo and Wistia from the same button, so you do not need to switch tools when a course mixes them. Password-protected Looms need the password entered first; see [how to download Loom videos from Skool](/blog/download-loom-videos-from-skool).

### Step 4: Do not forget community videos
In many communities the most useful material lives in the **Community** feed: weekly coaching calls, hot-seat reviews and guest workshops. Use Skool's search or your community's categories to find video posts, open each one, let the player load and click **Download**, then save the file into your replays folder.

### Step 5: Save attachments, then check and back up
- Save attached PDFs, spreadsheets and templates. For Notion or Google Docs templates, make your own copy.
- Copy any notes or links the instructor wrote below the videos.
- Spot-check a few MP4s in VLC or QuickTime to confirm they play.
- Copy the whole folder to an external drive or a private cloud folder.

## Keep Your Archive Personal

An offline archive is for **your own study** of content you had legitimate access to. Do not upload it, share folders with non-members, or resell it, and respect any rules your community has about downloads. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I download Skool videos after my membership has expired?
No. The extension works inside your logged-in session and only saves videos you can play. If your access has already ended you cannot open the locked lessons, so back up before it does.

### Can I download a whole course with one click?
No. Videos are saved lesson by lesson as you open each page. Set aside time for a long course and start early.

### What if a course mixes Skool videos with YouTube, Loom and Vimeo?
The extension detects Skool's native player, YouTube, Loom, Vimeo and Wistia and saves each as an MP4. Password-protected Looms need their password entered first.

### How much disk space will a course need?
It depends on video length and quality. As a rough guide, a 1080p lesson can take a few hundred MB per 20 minutes, so a 30-lesson course can take several GB. Check your free space before you start.

### Which plan should I choose to archive one course?
Try the free downloads first. If you need more, the Monthly plan gives unlimited downloads with no contract, so you can archive the course and cancel. Lifetime Pro suits people in several communities. See the [step-by-step download guide](/blog/how-to-download-skool-videos) to get started.
