### 4. `yt-dlp` + `ffmpeg` (Best Free DIY Option for Developers)
[yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp) is an open-source command-line downloader widely used by developers.
- **Cost:** $0, open source, and unlimited.
- **How it works on Skool:** Because Skool classrooms sit behind login authentication and serve signed Mux `.m3u8` manifests or domain-restricted iframes, passing a Skool page URL directly fails. You open Chrome DevTools (`F12`), filter the **Network** tab for `.m3u8` or `player`, copy the stream URL before its signed token expires, and run `yt-dlp --cookies-from-browser chrome --add-headers "Referer:https://www.skool.com/"`.
- **Best for:** Technical users comfortable with terminals and DevTools who want zero subscription cost. See our step-by-step tutorial in [yt-dlp vs a browser extension for Skool videos](/blog/yt-dlp-vs-extension-for-skool-videos).
