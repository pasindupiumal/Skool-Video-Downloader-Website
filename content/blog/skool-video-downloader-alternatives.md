### 4. `yt-dlp` + `ffmpeg` (Best Free DIY Option for Developers)
[yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp) is a widely used open-source command-line video downloader.
- **Cost:** Completely free and open source.
- **How it works on Skool:** Because Skool classrooms are private behind a login wall and use signed Mux `.m3u8` manifests or domain-restricted Vimeo/YouTube/Loom iframes, you cannot just pass a Skool URL to `yt-dlp` without authentication. You typically need to inspect the browser's **Network** tab (`F12`), filter for `.m3u8` or embed URLs, and pass `--cookies-from-browser chrome` to avoid `HTTP Error 403: Forbidden`.
- **Best for:** Developers and Linux/macOS power users who love the command line and are happy to extract stream URLs manually. See our side-by-side walkthrough in [yt-dlp vs a browser extension for Skool videos](/blog/yt-dlp-vs-extension-for-skool-videos).
